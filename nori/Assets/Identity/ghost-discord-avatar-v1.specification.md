# Ghost Discord avatar v1

## Identity

This avatar is an original symbolic representation of the operator callsign
Ghost. It does not use or imitate the face, eyes, clothing, or identity of the
friend shown in the separate 3D-reference photograph.

## Visual specification

The square avatar depicts an anonymous, nonhuman black-chrome operator mask
formed from volumetric particles and fine neural filaments. An original NORI
neural-brain insignia occupies the forehead and particle topology, with sparse
blue-frost signals traveling between regions. Curved field lines and small
energy nodes form a restrained magnetic-harness effect in the same depth field.

The palette uses near-black Ghost Purple: `#09060f`, `#1a0a2e`, a restrained
`#2d1052` midnight-purple sheen, limited `#6b2fa0` crest highlights, and crisp
blue-frost neural sparks. Purple reads almost black until light catches it.
Composition is centered head-and-shoulders with a circular-crop safety margin.

The artwork contains no text, watermark, human likeness, skull imagery,
weapons, existing media logos, green code, bright purple wash, or glowing white
eyes.
