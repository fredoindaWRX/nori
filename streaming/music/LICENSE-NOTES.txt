The published Synchronization and Master Use License permits StreamBeats tracks
to accompany YouTube/Twitch video content, royalty-free, worldwide, perpetually.
It supports monetized videos. Attribution is optional. This covers stream video
and retained YouTube VOD video; false Content ID claims can still happen.
Restrictions include standalone music redistribution, a music-only radio
station, remixing/sampling, and game/movie/TV use without separate licensing.
Use the tracks as background to the coding/demo stream, not a music channel.
